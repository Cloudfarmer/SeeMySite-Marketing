Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

SeeMySite ("we", "us", "our"), operated by SeeMySite Pty Ltd, provides roster and workforce management software, including GPS-verified site check-in, for businesses managing teams across one or more work sites. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how it's stored and protected, and what rights you have over it. We handle personal information in line with New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020.

Who this applies to

This policy covers two kinds of people who use SeeMySite:

  • Admins — people at a customer business who manage the account (setting up sites, workers, and schedules).
  • Workers — people added by an admin to check in and out of sites, view their roster, and report hazards or incidents.

Each customer business's data is kept fully separate from every other business using SeeMySite.

For workers, your employer decides which of your details are entered and who in their business can see them. We hold that information on their behalf to provide the service.

What we collect

Depending on your role, we collect:

  • Contact details — name, phone number, and email address (where provided), used to identify you and send invitations or notifications.
  • Location data — your device's GPS coordinates at the moment you check in or out of a site, used to verify your location against that site's registered address. We do not track your location at any other time.
  • Attendance and time records — check-in and check-out timestamps, used to generate timesheets.
  • Photos — images you choose to attach to a hazard, incident, or work-completion report.
  • Hazard and incident reports — any details you submit through the app.
  • Pay-related information — payable hours and pay types set by an admin, so timesheets can be exported to payroll software such as Xero or QuickBooks.
  • Worker files — documents an admin chooses to attach to a worker's record, such as contracts or letters, where that feature is switched on for that admin.
  • Account information — for admins, a login email and password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Billing information — for the admin who manages billing, payment is processed directly by our payment provider, Stripe. We do not store your card details ourselves.

SMS and text messaging

If you're a worker, your employer (an admin on SeeMySite) may send you a text message inviting you to use the app, or notifying you about your schedule. By providing your phone number to your employer for this purpose, you agree to receive these SMS messages from SeeMySite on their behalf. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of future messages at any time by replying STOP (where your phone network supports replies), or by asking your admin to remove your number.

We do not use your phone number for marketing, and we do not sell or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How we use your data

  • To verify site attendance and generate accurate timesheets.
  • To let admins build and manage rosters, assign tasks, and organize their team.
  • To alert relevant people to reported hazards or incidents.
  • To send account-related communications — invitations, password resets, and service notifications — by email or SMS.
  • To process billing for paid accounts.
  • To maintain and improve the security and reliability of the service.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run SeeMySite. We share only what each provider needs to perform its function:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores your account and app data.
  • Vercel — hosts the application and website.
  • Twilio — delivers SMS messages on our behalf.
  • Resend — delivers transactional emails on our behalf.
  • Stripe — processes payments for paid accounts.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, for any purpose.

Where your data is held

Some of these providers store or process data outside New Zealand. Our database and sign-in provider (Supabase) holds account and app data in Sydney, Australia. Our hosting provider (Vercel), text-message provider (Twilio), email provider (Resend) and payment provider (Stripe) operate mainly in the United States.

By using SeeMySite, your business and its workers acknowledge that personal information is stored and processed outside New Zealand. We take reasonable steps to make sure these providers protect it to a standard comparable to the safeguards in New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020, and we share with each only what it needs to do its job.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored on infrastructure provided by Supabase, with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Each business's data is logically isolated from every other business's data. Access within our own systems is restricted to what's needed to provide the service. If a privacy breach is likely to cause anyone serious harm, we will tell the people affected and the Privacy Commissioner, as the Privacy Act 2020 requires.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your business's account is active, so records like timesheets and hazard reports stay available for as long as they're useful. If a free trial ends and the account isn't upgraded, it becomes read-only for 60 days and is then closed. When an account is closed, we keep its data for 90 days in case you ask us to restore it, and then delete or anonymise it, except where the law requires us to keep it (for example, accounting records).

Your rights

Under New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020, you can ask us for the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. We'll respond within 20 working days. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights too, such as asking us to delete it or to object to certain uses of it. If you're a worker, many requests are best made to the admin at your employer first, since they control your account, but you're welcome to contact us directly as well, and we'll help however we can. If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (www.privacy.org.nz).

Questions about this policy, or want to exercise a data right? Email our privacy contact at support@seemysite.cloud.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the service evolves. We'll update the date at the top of this page when we do.
