Cleaning industry resources for managers

By Barry Williams, founder of SeeMySite · November 23, 2026

A short, hand-picked list of associations, safety guidance, pay rules and trade news for cleaning company managers in the US, UK, Australia and New Zealand.

ResourceWhat it’s for

ISSA Cleaning Industry Management Standard (CIMS)A certification standard for running a quality cleaning organization.
ISSA Cleaning Management InstituteTraining and certification courses for cleaning professionals and managers.
CleanLinkNews, guides and a product directory for building service contractors and in-house cleaning teams.
Cleaning & Maintenance Management (CMM)Magazine and news site on managing cleaning and facility maintenance operations.
NIOSH: Cleaning and Custodial ServicesOccupational health guidance for cleaners, custodial workers and their employers.

Washington L&I: Janitorial and HousekeepingCommon hazards, checklists and handouts for janitorial and housekeeping work.

ResourceWhat it’s for

ResourceWhat it’s for

About this list

These are independent resources we think cleaning company managers will find useful. SeeMySite isn’t affiliated with any of them, and nobody pays to be listed. Pay rates, laws and guidance change, so always check the official page for the current position. Links were checked on 23 November 2026.
