What a janitorial time tracking app should do

Janitorial crews work at sites you can’t watch: offices, clinics, schools and shops, often after hours. A good janitorial time tracking app answers three questions without a phone call:

  • Did they show up? Each cleaner checks in at the site and the check-in is tied to a location.
  • How long were they there? Check-in and check-out times give you the hours for each site.
  • Can I prove it? When a client says the work wasn’t done, you have a record of the visit.

Why paper timesheets and text messages fall short

A paper timesheet tells you what a cleaner says happened, days after the fact. A text saying “I’m here” can’t be checked. Neither helps at 8 am when the client calls about a missed clean, and both leave you re-keying hours at payroll time.

It’s a phone app that records when cleaners arrive at and leave each site. The best ones use GPS so each check-in is tied to a location, and show the manager who is on site.
