Software that works the way your crew works

SeeMySite is scheduling and time tracking for cleaning crews. I’m Barry Williams, and I’ve spent more than 25 years helping cleaning companies run their teams. This is built from what I’ve seen on the ground, not from a feature list.

Why SeeMySite exists

There is plenty of feature-rich scheduling software out there. Much of it is complicated to use, because it was designed by people who think like software designers. I believe software should be simple at the front, where your people use it every day, and keep the complexity at the back, where nobody has to see it.

That belief comes from a meeting with a software engineer. I asked why he had built a module in such a rigid, complicated way. His answer was, “Because that’s the most logical way.” I said, “But they don’t work that way. We need to build it how they work, not how we want it to work.” SeeMySite is built on that idea.

My background

I have worked in workforce management, scheduling and time-and-attendance software since 1999, mainly with cleaning companies. I started by talking directly with cleaning and facilities business owners in my home country of New Zealand, then moved into general management and channel management roles in Australia and the UK, with some involvement in taking these services to the United States. I have worked with hundreds of cleaning companies, from single owner-operators to large multinationals with multi-level reporting needs.

I have also worked as a product manager, designing how people who move between sites interact with their software, and what reporting companies of every size expect for the work they provide.

How it grew

SeeMySite began as a handful of screens that relied on basic texting. It has grown into a simple, interactive tool for crews that work across many sites, starting with cleaning and janitorial companies.
