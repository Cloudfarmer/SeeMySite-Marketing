Roster and Workforce Management — Made simple!

SeeMySite is roster and workforce management software built for teams large or small, designed to work across multiple locations anywhere in the world. We know your staff want something easy to use. We also know mobility is key — and that's what SeeMySite offers.

SeeMySite works for small organisations right through to the largest, using a custom-designed multi-node (levels) architecture — so adding, sharing, and moving staff around becomes easy. Staff are invited by you to use our PWA (Progressive Web Application), where the magic happens.

Geography was never the hard part.

SeeMySite doesn't care which country your sites are in. It was built that way from the start, not bolted on afterwards.

GPS works exactly the same everywhere

A site in rural Otago verifies exactly the same way as one in downtown Toronto — it's your phone's own GPS, not a local address lookup that might not cover where you are.

Billed in your own currency

Pricing is set in USD, but you're charged in your local currency automatically at checkout — no manual conversion, no surprises.

Nothing to install locally

It's a website that behaves like an app. If a worker has a phone and a browser, they're covered — no country-specific app store, no local server.
