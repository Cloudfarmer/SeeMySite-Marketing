What cleaning companies need from time and attendance software

Contract cleaning teams work across many sites, often in the evening or early morning with no supervisor present. Time and attendance software for cleaning companies should let you:

  • Verify attendance: cleaners check in at the site, and the check-in is tied to a location.
  • See who is late or missing: know who has arrived and who hasn’t while there is still time to arrange cover.
  • Record hours by site: a clear record of time on site to check before you run payroll.
  • Settle client queries: a record of each visit when a customer says a clean was missed.

The problem with paper timesheets and WhatsApp

Paper timesheets and group messages tell you what happened after the fact, if they tell you at all. They are hard to check, slow to total and offer little when a client rings to complain. For a company with a handful of sites and a small team, that is where complaints, lost contracts and unpaid hours come from.
